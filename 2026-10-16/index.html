On 15 October three seeds went out inside the normal daily flow with real copy, one from each of the two sending domains and one from the frozen domain, and were read automatically.

03

The sequence was rebuilt and every message is now written per lead

A new structure went into production for the first and second steps. The first email now opens on where the company was actually found, computed from the list the record came from rather than written freely, then the line on what your side solves, then a small offer and a yes or no question. The second opens on a horizon question, carries the proof for its segment and repeats that same line. The self introduction came out of both. The day's queue was first rewritten by hand against the new structure and approved before sending, then the drafter itself moved onto the new rules so that no two messages are identical, with fixed templates kept only as a fallback. The first queue written under the new rules sent on 14 October. The automated review was rewritten to match: a draft that introduces itself, cites a source other than the one on its own record, or offers something that is not on offer is held out of the day rather than sent.
